📖How It Works — Event Manager Guide
▼
1
Create a League
Fill in the league name, day, start date, season length, format, team size, handicap, points system, holes per week, and rotation. Set Win/Tie/Loss/Show Up points in Points Config. Click + Create League. The full season schedule generates automatically.
2
Edit League Settings
Select your league under Manage League — all settings display at the top. Click ✏️ Edit Settings to update any setting at any time including name, format, handicap, points config, and rotation.
3
Print Signup Form
Click 🖨 Print Signup Form to open a clean printable sheet with numbered rows for team name, player names, handicap, and phone. Corner markers guarantee accurate AI scanning. Print and hand out to players to fill in.
4
Add Players / Scan Signup Sheet
Scan the completed signup sheet with AI (photo of handwritten or printed sheet — names and handicaps fill in automatically) or add players manually. Each card has team name, handicap, starting hole, player names, and season points.
5
Generate Season Schedule
Once all teams are entered click 🗓 Generate Schedule. Builds a full round-robin — every team plays every other team — spread across all 22 weeks automatically. Or scan a printed schedule instead using the Scan Schedule button.
6
Weekly Setup — Shotgun Start
Each week enter the week number and date. Click 🎲 Auto-Assign Holes to randomly assign shotgun starting holes, or adjust manually. Click 💾 Save then ▶ Set Active to activate the week.
7
Players Submit Scores
Players use ⛳ Score Entry, select their league, week, and team. Scorecard loads with starting hole highlighted in yellow. Enter scores and hit Submit. Once submitted the scorecard is locked — only the admin can make changes.
8
Points Auto-Calculate
When a score is submitted the system finds the matchup, compares net scores and awards points automatically.
Win = 2+1 = 3ptsTie = 1+1 = 2ptsLoss = 0+1 = 1ptNo Show = 0ptsForfeit Win = 3pts
9
Event Manager Edit Scores
Only the event manager can edit scores after submission. Go to Manage League → Edit Submitted Scores, select the week, and all scorecards show hole by hole. Edit any score and click 💾 Save Score — points recalculate automatically.
10
Print Weekly Sheet
Enter the week number in Weekly Setup then click 🖨 Print Standings. Opens a printable page showing this week's matchup results (gross, HCP, net, win/tie/loss, points) plus the full season standings. Ready to post on the wall each week.
11
End of Season
Print the final standings sheet for your records. Use 🗑 Delete League only after recording final standings — this permanently removes all players, scores, and history and cannot be undone.
